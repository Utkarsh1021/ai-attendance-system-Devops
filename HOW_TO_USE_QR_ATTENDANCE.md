# How to Use QR-Based Attendance 📱

## For Students 👨‍🎓

### **Step-by-Step Guide:**

#### **1. Login to Your Account**
- Go to the attendance system homepage
- Select "Student" role
- Click "Sign In"
- Enter your registration number and password
- Click "Sign In"

#### **2. Access Your Dashboard**
After logging in, you'll see your profile with:
- Your name
- Section
- Email
- Registration number

#### **3. Mark Attendance Using QR Code**

**Click the button:**
```
📷 Scan QR & Mark Attendance
```

**This will open the QR Scanner**

#### **4. Scan the QR Code**
- Point your phone's back camera at the QR code displayed by your faculty
- Hold steady until the QR code is detected
- The scanner will automatically read the code

**Tips for better scanning:**
- Hold your device steady
- Ensure good lighting
- Keep QR code within the frame
- Avoid glare on the screen

#### **5. Verify Your Face**
After scanning, the face capture screen will open:
- Position your face within the oval guide
- Make sure your face is well-lit
- Click "Verify & Mark Attendance"
- Wait for the AI to recognize your face

#### **6. Confirmation**
- If successful, you'll see: "Attendance marked successfully! ✓"
- The modal will close automatically
- Your attendance history will update

## For Faculty 👨‍🏫

### **How to Generate QR Codes:**

#### **1. Login to Faculty Portal**
- Go to `/faculty`

#### **2. Create an Attendance Session**
- Click "Create New Session"
- Enter session details:
  - Subject name
  - Section
  - Date and time
- Click "Create Session"

#### **3. Display QR Code**
- The QR code will be generated automatically
- Display it on your screen or projector
- Students can scan it with their phones

#### **4. Monitor Attendance**
- View real-time attendance as students scan
- See who has marked attendance
- Export attendance reports

## Troubleshooting 🔧

### **QR Scanner Issues:**

**Problem:** Camera not working
- **Solution:** Allow camera permissions in your browser
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy → Camera → Allow
- **Safari:** Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow

**Problem:** QR code not detected
- **Solution:** 
  - Ensure good lighting
  - Hold phone steady
  - Move closer/farther from QR code
  - Clean your camera lens

**Problem:** "Invalid QR code" error
- **Solution:** 
  - Make sure you're scanning the correct QR code
  - Ask faculty to regenerate the QR code
  - Check if the session is still active

### **Face Recognition Issues:**

**Problem:** Face not recognized
- **Solution:**
  - Ensure your face is well-lit
  - Remove glasses/mask if possible
  - Position face within the oval guide
  - Try again with better lighting

**Problem:** "Face does not match logged-in student"
- **Solution:**
  - Make sure you're logged in with your own account
  - Don't try to mark attendance for someone else
  - Contact admin if you believe this is an error

**Problem:** "Attendance already marked"
- **Solution:**
  - You've already marked attendance for this session
  - Check your attendance history to confirm

**Problem:** "You are not authorized for this section"
- **Solution:**
  - The QR code is for a different section
  - Make sure you're in the correct class
  - Contact faculty if you believe this is an error

## Security Features 🔐

### **What Makes This Secure:**

1. **QR Code Expiration**
   - QR codes expire after 4 seconds
   - Prevents screenshot sharing
   - Ensures real-time attendance

2. **Face Recognition**
   - Verifies your identity
   - Prevents proxy attendance
   - Matches against registered face

3. **Section Validation**
   - Only students in the correct section can mark attendance
   - Prevents unauthorized access

4. **Duplicate Prevention**
   - Can't mark attendance twice for the same session
   - Prevents gaming the system

## FAQ ❓

### **Q: Can I mark attendance from home?**
**A:** No, the QR code is only displayed in the classroom. You must be physically present to scan it.

### **Q: What if I forget to scan the QR code?**
**A:** Contact your faculty immediately. They may be able to mark your attendance manually.

### **Q: Can I use this on a desktop/laptop?**
**A:** Yes, but it works best on mobile devices with cameras. Desktop webcams can be used but may be less convenient.

### **Q: What if my phone camera is broken?**
**A:** Contact your faculty for alternative attendance marking methods.

### **Q: How long does the QR code stay active?**
**A:** The QR code refreshes every 4 seconds for security. Make sure to scan it while it's displayed.

### **Q: Can I mark attendance for my friend?**
**A:** No, face recognition prevents proxy attendance. Each student must mark their own attendance.

### **Q: What if the face recognition fails?**
**A:** Try again with better lighting. If it continues to fail, contact your faculty or admin.

### **Q: Can I see my attendance history?**
**A:** Yes, your attendance history is displayed on your dashboard after logging in.
